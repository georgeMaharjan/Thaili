# Thaili 1.4.8

## Updates and app access

- Check for, download, and start installing Android updates from inside Thaili. Android still asks you to confirm installation.
- Choose fingerprint or your phone PIN, pattern, or password when unlocking the app.
- App lock waits for your choice instead of opening an authentication prompt automatically.

## Accounts and categories

- Edit an account using its current balance, with account number and institution fields hidden when they are not needed.
- Browse more category icons, including icons for transfers, income, expenses, loans, and lending.
- Find the account screen from the Home balance card.

## Insights and Settings

- See income and expenses first, followed by budget use and top spending categories.
- Review savings and spending patterns in grouped sections, with a clearer budget progress bar.
- Find settings under Money plan, App & alerts, and Data & privacy.

## Daily review reminder

- Schedule the 9:00 review reminder without requesting Android’s exact-alarm access.
